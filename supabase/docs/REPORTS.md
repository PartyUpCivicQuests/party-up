# Reading the Reports tab

Reports answer two questions: **which quests land**, and **what to do next**. All numbers are anonymous totals.

## The core number: finish rate
Finishes divided by the members who could see the quest (everyone in its guild, or the guilds under it, who had joined by the time it closed). Opens are counted too, but they overcount a little, so finish rate is the one to trust.

Quests seen by fewer than 10 members are marked "small sample" and left out of comparisons.

## Running now: boost what's lagging
Each live quest is compared with how quests of the same kind usually build up. Timed waves use your own typical first-hour curve; evergreen quests use how far into the week it is. If a quest is under 70% of its usual pace, it's **Behind pace**, and **Second call** reposts it to Discord once.

## Strong and lagging quests
A quest is compared with the average of its own kind (amplify with amplify, recruit with recruit), because some kinds are naturally harder. Eight points above average counts as strong; eight below counts as lagging.
- **Run again** copies a strong quest into the composer.
- **Rework it** copies a lagging one so you can change the platform, timing, caption, or add a voice note.

## What works
Average finish rate by kind of quest, by platform, and by start time, with how many quests each is based on (`n`). Don't read much into anything with n under 3.

## Evergreen library health
For library quests run four or more times, recent runs are compared with early ones. Ten or more points down usually means people are tired of it: **Rewrite it** or **Retire** it, and autopilot will skip retired ones.

## Export
**Export CSV** downloads one row per quest with every number above, for spreadsheets or sharing with a coalition partner.
