# Principles

Party Up adapts the *Mini-Manual of Informational Insurgency* for US organizing over many years. These rules are built into the code. Please keep them when you change it.

## Real people, real accounts, by hand
- Every like, share, save, comment, and search is done by a real person on their own account. Party Up opens the post; the person does the rest.
- No bots, scripts, auto-liking, account farms, multiple accounts per person, or bought engagement. Platforms ban accounts for this in waves, which would hand the opposition the "attack by the platform" the manual warns about.
- XP, titles, and buttons are the only rewards. Never money, prizes, or gift cards: paying for engagement crosses platform rules and possibly election law.
- XP is capped per quest per person, and leaderboards count quests finished rather than raw likes, so there's nothing to gain from extra accounts.

## Where quests point
- Facebook, Instagram, YouTube, and TikTok reach the most people outside our bubble. X, Threads, and Bluesky are on the list too. Group chats are where bubbles actually break.
- **Reddit is left out on purpose.** Sending a group to vote or comment on a specific Reddit thread is "brigading," which Reddit's site-wide rules prohibit. A member sharing our own explainer in a community they already belong to, in their own words, within that community's rules, is fine, but that isn't a quest.
- Never point a quest at the opposition's posts. The Watchtower is view-only. Ignoring their posts (not blocking, not replying, not hate-watching) is what starves them of reach. That's the weekly Vow of Silence.

## Truth and the record
- Every quest an organizer writes needs a source link.
- Quests that make a factual claim about a named person need a second organizer's sign-off before they go out.
- Nothing is deleted. Mistakes get a visible correction that members see on the quest and organizers see in the archive. In Brazil, the opposition built its court case by cataloging the channel's own messages; a clean, corrected record is the best defense.
- No realistic AI-generated images or video of real people. Minnesota and several other states restrict election deepfakes, and authentic phone footage performs better anyway.

## Privacy
- Members join with an invite code and a name. Email is optional and used only for sign-in links.
- No contact-book uploads. Members invite people with a personal link or QR code.
- The record of who finished which quest is deleted a day after each quest closes. Reports use anonymous totals only, and there's no per-person drill-down.

## Sustainable pace
- Weekly streaks, not daily. Streak shields cover a missed week.
- Each member sets the most quests they want per week. The schedule shows organizers when a week is getting crowded.
- Evergreen quests carry quiet weeks so organizers can rest. Autopilot fills the board from the library but never fires a timed wave on its own.
- Timed waves get a freshness check, so stale quests are held instead of sent.

*None of this is legal advice. Talk to an election lawyer before a public launch.*
